Hey, I'm Warre! I am a technical artist with a soft spot for Houdini.\
I enjoy plenty of topics ranging from real-time shaders to NPR-rendering and unique smoke-and-mirror solutions.\
Basically anything from games to films and everything in between that needs some artistic problem solving. :D\

In June of 2026, I graduated with a bachelors at Howest Digital Arts and Entertainment as a 3D game artist (with a focus on environment and tech art).\
Currently I am doing my masters at Breda University of Applied Sciences, following the Masters Game Technology course.\

Over the course one year I will create a research paper that fills a knowledge gap somewhere around the "game technology"-field.\
Currently while writing this it is week 2 and I am doing preliminary studies on what would be useful topics to look into.\

I'm deciding between:\
1) An Adaptive Lattice that would have some kind of LOD and/or localized detail-system which would help real-time applications in using a Lattice more often.\
2) A faster straight skeleton algorithm. Or an approximation of it at least. I'd like to see if I can balance a slight loss in quality with a faster calculation time.\
3) Gridless Cellular Automata. I haven't researched this a lot yet, I'd bet money on it that this already exists, but I want to look into this and see if this could open up doors for creative solutions down the line.\

If you want to follow me on this journey, make sure to check in on this blog every once in a while!\
I will upload an update on my research every other week.\
I'll also share some fun Tips & Tricks that I have learned over the years, whenever I have some spare time.\
